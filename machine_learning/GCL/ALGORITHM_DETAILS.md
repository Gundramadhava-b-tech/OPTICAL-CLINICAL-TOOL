# GCL: Ganglion Cell Layer

## Anatomical Significance
The **Ganglion Cell Layer (GCL)** contains the cell bodies of the retinal ganglion cells.

## AI Segmentation Logic
- **Identification**: A hypo-reflective (darker) layer located between the RNFL and the IPL.
- **Features**: Highly sensitive to neurodegenerative changes.
- **Metrics**: Volume analysis of the Ganglion Cell Complex (GCC) often combines GCL, IPL, and RNFL for comprehensive glaucoma screening.
