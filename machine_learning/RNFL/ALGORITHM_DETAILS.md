# RNFL: Retinal Nerve Fiber Layer

## Anatomical Significance
The **Retinal Nerve Fiber Layer (RNFL)** consists of the unmyelinated axons of retinal ganglion cells (RGC) that converge to form the optic nerve.

## AI Segmentation Logic
- **Identification**: Hyper-reflective band immediately posterior to the ILM.
- **Features**: Varies in thickness based on distance from the optic disc (ISNT rule).
- **Clinical Marker**: Thinning of the RNFL is a primary structural indicator of **Glaucoma**.
- **Metrics**: AI extracts mean thickness profiles to compare against normative databases.
