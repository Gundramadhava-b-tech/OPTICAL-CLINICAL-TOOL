# ONL: Outer Nuclear Layer

## Anatomical Significance
The **Outer Nuclear Layer (ONL)** contains the cell bodies of the photoreceptor cells (rods and cones).

## AI Segmentation Logic
- **Identification**: The thickest hypo-reflective (dark) layer in the outer retina.
- **Topology**: Located between the OPL and the hyper-reflective External Limiting Membrane (ELM) / IS/OS junction.
- **Clinical Marker**: Atrophy of the ONL indicates permanent loss of photoreceptors.
