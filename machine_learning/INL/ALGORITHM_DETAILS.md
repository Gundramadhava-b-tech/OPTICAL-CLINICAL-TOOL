# INL: Inner Nuclear Layer

## Anatomical Significance
The **Inner Nuclear Layer (INL)** contains the cell bodies of horizontal cells, bipolar cells, amacrine cells, and Müller cells.

## AI Segmentation Logic
- **Identification**: A distinct hypo-reflective (dark) layer between the hyper-reflective IPL and OPL.
- **Clinical Marker**: Thickening or presence of cystic spaces in this layer is associated with **Macular Edema**.
- **Topology**: $IPL \prec INL \prec OPL$.
