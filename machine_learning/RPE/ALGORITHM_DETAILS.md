# RPE: Retinal Pigment Epithelium

## Anatomical Significance
The **Retinal Pigment Epithelium (RPE)** is a single layer of pigmented cells that supports the photoreceptors. It forms part of the blood-retinal barrier.

## AI Segmentation Logic
- **Identification**: Characterized by a very strong hyper-reflective band at the posterior edge of the retina.
- **Topology**: It is the outermost layer of the retina ($ONL \prec RPE$).
- **Clinical Marker**: Irregularities (drusen) or thinning of the RPE are hallmarks of **Age-related Macular Degeneration (AMD)**.
- **Inference**: AI detects the RPE floor to calculate total retinal thickness and detect geographic atrophy.
