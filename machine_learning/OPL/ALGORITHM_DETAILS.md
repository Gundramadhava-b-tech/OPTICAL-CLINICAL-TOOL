# OPL: Outer Plexiform Layer

## Anatomical Significance
The **Outer Plexiform Layer (OPL)** is a synaptic layer where photoreceptor cells (rods and cones) synapse with bipolar and horizontal cells.

## AI Segmentation Logic
- **Identification**: Hyper-reflective band between the hypo-reflective INL and ONL.
- **Topology**: $INL \prec OPL \prec ONL$.
- **Note**: The OPL/ONL boundary can be difficult to delineate in low-quality scans due to Henle's fiber layer.
