# ILM: Internal Limiting Membrane

## Anatomical Significance
The **Internal Limiting Membrane (ILM)** is the boundary between the retina and the vitreous body. It is the innermost layer of the retina and consists of a basement membrane formed by the footplates of Müller cells.

## AI Segmentation Logic
- **Identification**: Characterized as the first high-contrast interface encountered from the anterior side of the B-scan.
- **Features**: Highly hyper-reflective in healthy eyes.
- **Topological Constraint**: Must be the anterior-most layer ($ILM \prec RNFL$).
- **Clinical Marker**: Used to detect Epiretinal Membranes (ERM) and Macular Holes.
